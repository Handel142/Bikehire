Business settings

The standard price is used by every pick-up point that has not set its own price (operators can set their own, or you can set it under Operators). A new price applies to bikes leased from now on; rentals already out keep theirs. The price is per hour. A student pays for the time booked, even if they return early. If they keep the bike past the grace minutes, the time overdue is added at the same hourly rate, worked out to the minute and rounded to the nearest shilling.
